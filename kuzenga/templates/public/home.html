KUZENGA QUESTIONS

School administration, explained.

What is Kuzenga?

Kuzenga is Elevon Digital's school management system for schools in Zimbabwe. It brings student records, admissions, academic administration and controlled school finance workflows into one school workspace.

Who is Kuzenga designed for?

Kuzenga is designed for school administrators, teachers and finance staff. Each person uses the modules and permissions assigned to their role and the school's subscription.

What school administration problems does Kuzenga address?

Kuzenga helps organise student information, application reviews, enrolment, classes and financial records that can otherwise be spread across separate files. School roles and tenant boundaries control access to records.

How does Kuzenga help manage school fees?

The implemented finance workflows support fee structures, student billing, invoices, recorded payments, payment allocation, outstanding balances and receipts. Finance access depends on the school's enabled modules and the user's permissions.

How does Kuzenga help school administrators?

Authorised administrators can manage school profiles, academic years, terms, classes, subjects, student records and admissions workflows. Available actions depend on their role and the school's enabled modules.

How can schools request a demonstration?

Use the demo enquiry form, email sales@elevondigitalzw.com, or contact us on WhatsApp. The team can discuss your school's requirements and the implemented modules.
